Troubleshoot

Try these quick fixes before you contact support. Tap a problem to open the steps.

The Insert money button keeps loading
  1. Check the LAN cable in port 1 of the Mikrotik router to see if it lights up. Try changing the LAN cable if it doesn't.
  2. Make sure the ISP (the main router that has the internet connection) has internet and its LAN ports are working.
  3. Look at the NodeMCU (the board next to the Mikrotik router) and check that it has blue lights.
  4. If everything is working, try to follow the guide video.
Watch the guide video
Customers cannot see the portal page
  1. Forget the WiFi network on the phone and connect again.
  2. Open the browser and type 10.10.10.1 in the address bar.
  3. Check that the controller board and router are both powered.
  4. Restart the PisoWiFi vendo.
Time is not added after a coin is inserted
  1. Check the cable between the coin slot and the controller board.
  2. Make sure the coin slot is set to the correct pulse value.
  3. Restart the controller board.
  4. Try to replace the adaptor connected to the controller board.
  5. If it still fails, send us a photo of the wiring.

Still not working? Contact support to reflash the NodeMCU

  1. Message or call support (see Customer Support below) and say you need the NodeMCU reflashed.
  2. Prepare a laptop.
  3. Prepare a micro USB cable.
  4. Prepare a LAN cable.
  5. Keep the NodeMCU and the controller board within reach so support can guide you step by step.
Internet is slow or keeps disconnecting
  1. Test your internet line by plugging a laptop directly into the modem. Plug the internet line into port 4 of the router only. Ports 2 and 3 of the router are only for the AP.
  2. Check the LAN cable and replace it if it is damaged.
  3. Move the access point away from metal and thick walls.
  4. Go to the JuanFi portal and check the promo rates. Follow the picture below.
